Data & Security

How Pension Planner stores your data

Local scenario storage, backups and device transfer.

Local storage by default

The scenario library is stored in the app’s private Application Support area using versioned JSON persistence. A previous valid local file is retained as a recovery backup when saving.

iPhone and Mac are separate

The two apps share modelling logic, but live scenario libraries are separate. Pension Planner does not automatically synchronise scenarios through iCloud or a Pension Planner cloud service.

Manual backup transfer

Settings → Data can export the complete scenario library. Import validates the file and replaces the receiving device’s local scenario library; it is not a merge.

What backups may contain

  • people and dates of birth;
  • assets and DC pensions;
  • GIA balances, pooled acquisition costs and dividend assumptions;
  • DB, State Pension, annuity, rental-property and other income;
  • spending and Retirement Budgets;
  • capital events, contributions, tax-year precision inputs and assumptions.

Exported reports

PDF reports are generated locally and may contain sensitive retirement assumptions and financial amounts.

No financial-account connection

The current app does not connect directly to banks, pension providers or investment platforms and does not import live credentials or transactions.

PPension Planner

Pension Planner is a retirement modelling and decision-support application. It does not provide regulated financial, investment, pension or tax advice.

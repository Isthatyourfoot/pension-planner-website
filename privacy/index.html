Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026.

Privacy-first design: Pension Planner does not require an online account. Retirement scenarios are stored locally unless you deliberately export or share a backup or report.

Information entered into the app

You may enter names, dates of birth, pension and investment balances, income, spending, tax assumptions, retirement dates, capital events and other information needed for modelling.

The current app stores this information locally in the application’s private storage area and does not automatically upload it to a Pension Planner server.

Backups, PDFs and sharing

Exported backup files and PDF reports are created at your request. If you AirDrop, email, save to cloud storage or otherwise share a file, that copy is handled by the destination you chose.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current app does not use advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. If this changes, this policy and App Store disclosures will be updated.

Support communications

If you email support@pensionplannerapp.co.uk, information in the message is used to respond to your enquiry and investigate the reported issue.

Website data

This static site contains no advertising or analytics code and sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical logs for security and delivery under its own terms.

Data retention

Scenario data remains on your device until you edit/delete it, remove the app and its data, or replace the local library through an import.

Children

Pension Planner is designed for adults undertaking retirement planning and is not directed at children.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@pensionplannerapp.co.uk.

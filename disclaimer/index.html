Important information

Disclaimer and modelling limitations

Please read this before relying on a Pension Planner result.

Pension Planner is not regulated financial advice. It is a what-if modelling and decision-support tool. It does not recommend that you buy, sell, transfer, crystallise or withdraw from any particular financial product.

No forecast is a promise

Investment returns, inflation, longevity, spending, tax law and pension rules are uncertain. A deterministic or Monte Carlo result describes outcomes generated by the assumptions entered into the model; it is not a guarantee or a known real-world probability.

Tax modelling is simplified

Pension Planner models selected UK retirement-tax concepts for planning purposes. It is not a tax-return calculator and does not reproduce every HMRC rule, relief, election, transitional protection or provider-specific treatment.
